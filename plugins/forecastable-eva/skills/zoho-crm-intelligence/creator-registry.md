# Zoho CRM Creator Registry

Version: 2026-09-29. Companion to `zoho-crm-intelligence.md`. Labels: VENDOR (Zoho owned), PARTNER (Zoho partner or consultancy), INDEPENDENT (no confirmed Zoho partner status). Ranking rule: usefulness to an admin or analyst who navigates Zoho CRM and pulls data out of it; audience size ignored. "~" dates are estimated from YouTube relative dates.

## Tiers

- **Tier 1, check monthly and read first:** ranks 1 to 5 (Zoho developer docs, Kaizen, Zenatta, Zoho main channel, Zoho MCP docs). COQL, API and MCP answers live here.
- **Tier 2, check monthly for new how-to:** ranks 6 to 15 (Help Center and Community, Drew Brockbank, @ZohoCRMOfficial, Zoho Developer Hangout, Lior Izik, CRMOZ, OfficeHub Tech, Zocademy, Codroid Labs, Avon Collis / Relevate).
- **Tier 3, check quarterly:** ranks 16 to 25. Mostly partner marketing, AU context, or slower channels.

## Notes on the ranking

- Ranks 1, 2, 4 and 5 are where COQL, API v8 and MCP answers actually live. For anything about data extraction, start there and use partners for worked examples.
- Zenatta is the single most useful non-vendor source: it covers CRM for Everyone, reports, Blueprint, Deluge and MCP with 2026 content, and the weekly CRM Zen Show tracks feature changes.
- Independent creators are thin. Most "independent" Zoho CRM channels found are content farms producing 2 to 3 minute "[2026 Guide]" videos (Guides With Garry, BM Tech Tutorials, Tutorialjake, CRM Tutorials Library). They were excluded as low value.
- Australian coverage is weak on YouTube depth. Avon Collis (Relevate) is the most substantive AU creator; Zoho ANZ, It's Solved and Smartmates are mostly promotional.
- Target Cloud is UK based (targetcloud.co.uk), not Australian.
