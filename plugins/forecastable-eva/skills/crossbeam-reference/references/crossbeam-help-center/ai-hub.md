### WHAT ARE CROSSBEAM CREDITS?
Crossbeam Credits measure AI access to your ecosystem data. When an AI tool or agent, like Claude, ChatGPT, or a custom agent, pulls partner and account intelligence from Crossbeam, it draws credits from your organization's shared balance.

Credits are pooled across your whole team, not assigned per user, so anyone's AI workflows pull from the same balance.


### HOW CREDITS WORK
What consumes credits today?
Starting September 1, 2026, credits will meter access to Crossbeam's MCP server, the connection that lets AI tools and agents easily work with your Crossbeam data. MCP is where credits apply today. Over time, credits may expand to meter other ways AI works with your Crossbeam data. Customers will be notified with a healthy notice period ahead of any changes.

How many credits are included in my plan?
Every plan, even our Free plan, receives a baseline amount of credits included within your platform fee. Here's the breakdown:
| Plan | Annual Credits |
| Free | 50/yr |
| Connector | 500/yr |
| Supernode | 2,500/yr |
| Enterprise | 5,000/yr |


#### A few things worth knowing:
- These credit amounts are the baseline credits your plan receives for the full year
- Credits reset every year and don't roll over
- Baseline credits are provided within your platform fee If your plan does not currently include the annual platform fee, please reach out to our support team for steps on accessing MCP and other AI capabilities.
- If your plan does not currently include the annual platform fee, please reach out to our support team for steps on accessing MCP and other AI capabilities.
- Supernode and Enterprise customers can buy additional packs whenever they need them. See the Buying more credits section below for additional details.
- If you're on our Free or Connector plan and hit your limit, paid AI features are paused until your annual reset. If that's happening regularly, it might be time to upgrade. See the Buying more credits section below for additional details.
- If you need help determining the credits you think you’ll need, reach out to your Account Manager or Crossbeam Support (email: support@crossbeam.com ) <!-- VALIDATE-OK[person-address]: verbatim public Crossbeam Help Center text -->

When will Crossbeam begin enforcing credit limits?
Crossbeam will begin enforcing credit limits on all plans starting September 1, 2026 . Right now, credit limits are not enforced, you can run over your plan allocation without interruption.

Are credits shared across the team?
Yes, credits are shared across your team. There are no per-user allocations or caps, anyone with a qualifying seat can draw from the balance.

Do I need a seat to use credits and AI functionality like MCP?
Yes, you'll need a Full Access or Sales seat to connect Crossbeam's MCP server to an LLM like Claude or ChatGPT. Admins can also control MCP access by role in Crossbeam. Manage this from Settings > Roles & Permissions . Click Edit next to a role, locate the MCP permission, and set it to either Access or No Access . All roles default to Access .

What happens if I run out of credits?
You're not completely cut off. Free AI capabilities keep working, but you lose access to paid AI features until your credits reset at the next annual billing cycle or you purchase additional credit packs. Free capabilities include things like asking about best practices, your own account context, or any sort of product knowledge requests.

Does MCP usage count toward my Record Exports limit?
No. MCP activity only draws from credits, it has no impact on your Record Export limit. However, as MCP capabilities advance, this may change over time. You will be notified with a healthy notice period if this changes.


### BUYING MORE CREDITS
Can I buy more credits?
Yes! Supernode and Enterprise customers have the option to purchase additional credits over the baseline credit amount included within your plan. Free and Connector customers are not eligible for credit pack purchases at this time.
| Plan | Annual Credits | Can I Buy More? |
| Free | 50/yr | No |
| Connector | 500/yr | No |
| Supernode | 2,500/yr | Yes |
| Enterprise | 5,000/yr | Yes |
How many credits can I purchase?
Supernode and Enterprise customers can purchase as many credits as you think your team will need. Credits are sold in packs and can be combined to fit your specific needs.

Credit packs available include:
| Pack | Credits | Cost |
| 10K pack | 10,000 | $1,000 |
| 25K pack | 25,000 | $2,500 |
| 100k pack | 100,000 | $9,000 (10% discount) |
How much does a credit cost?
Credits have a flat fee cost of $.10/credit.

How do I get more credits?
Supernode and Enterprise customers can add credits two ways:
- Self-serve: Go to Plan & Billing and click Add more credits . Select a credit pack (10K, 25K, or 100K) and confirm you're authorized to make purchases for your organization. Credits apply immediately.

- Order Form (OF): Work with your Account Manager to add a credit pack to your contract, or email support@crossbeam.com <!-- VALIDATE-OK[person-address]: verbatim public Crossbeam Help Center text -->
✍️ Note Only Crossbeam admins with access to Plan & Billing can purchase credits through the app. Self-serve credit packs renew automatically with your subscription . An invoice is sent to your organization's billing contact using your existing payment terms.

Do unused purchased credits roll over?
No, all credits (plan-included and purchased packs) reset at your annual renewal. What you don't use cannot be rolled over.


### WHEN ARE CREDITS USED (AND WHEN ARE THEY NOT)
Free capabilities include things like asking about best practices, your own account context, or any sort of product knowledge requests. Some actions are always free. Others draw from your credit balance. The difference depends on the type of request and how much data work (compute) is involved.
| Usage pattern | Credits per user / month | What it looks like | Typically |
| Light | ~200 | Account lookups before calls, ad-hoc overlap checks, quick partner context | Individual sales reps |
| Moderate | ~600 | Partner recommendations, ecosystem lists, light automation | Partnership managers |
| Heavy | ~1,000 | Scheduled ecosystem scans, automated enrichment, batch reporting | RevOps and always-on automation |
How do I know how many credits I might use?
Usage estimates depend on how your team works. A good way to estimate: think in terms of patterns of work, then scale by your team.

A person doing mostly lookups (account checks before calls, ad-hoc overlap checks) runs around 200 credits/month. This is likely your individual sales reps.
Someone in daily workflows (partner recommendations, ecosystem reports, light automation) runs around 600/month. This is likely your partnership managers.

An always-on automation (scheduled scans, enrichment, batch jobs) runs around 1,000/month. This is likely coming from your RevOps team.

Multiply by your mix, and you've got a working estimate. The bigger takeaways: credits pool across your whole account, so light users and power users draw from one shared balance. If you’d like to dig into your mix or use case, reach out to your Account Manager or our support team ( support@crossbeam.com ). <!-- VALIDATE-OK[person-address]: verbatim public Crossbeam Help Center text -->


### UNDERSTANDING CREDIT USAGE
How do I track my usage?
Admins can monitor credit consumption and overall usage from the Plan & Billing page, broken down by MCP tool calls and active users by week, month, or all time. Only admins have access to the org-level usage view.

Will I receive notifications when I’m approaching my credit limit?
Yes! We have a variety of ways Admins will be notified when approaching credit limits:
Via email:
Customers will receive email notifications at 75%, 90%, and 100% of available credit usage.

In-app:
Customers will also see a notification banner within their Crossbeam instance to indicate when you’re approaching credit limits.
