Enterprise organizations need precise control over partnership data access to ensure sensitive information isn’t visible by default. This feature allows Revenue Leaders and Partnership Managers to grant visibility only to select users based on roles or need-to-know criteria, enhancing data security and fostering collaboration.
This feature is available by request only.
Please contact your CSM to learn more.
✍️ Note
You must be an Admin in Crossbeam to manage user access to data in Crossbeam.

### Definitions of user Access
There are three types of access levels to select from:
- Manage Access: Allows users to view and manage access to all partnerships.
- View Access: Grants visibility to partnerships. Access can be further customized at the partner level.
- Per-Partner Access: Restricts visibility entirely unless the user is explicitly granted access to a specific partner, or they created the partnership themselves.

### How to Configure Access Levels

#### Global Configuration by Role
In Crossbeam, click on the Settings Icon, and select Role & Permissions from the side panel. Select a specific role by clicking on Edit . Updates here will automatically apply to all existing and new users assigned to the role.

Click Save Changes when done.


#### At the Partner Level Configuration
In Crossbeam, click on the Partners Icon, click on a Partner, and select the Sharing Settings . In the modal, click on Team Access and adjust the roles or add people to edit access.

Click Close when done.

Attribution in Crossbeam helps you understand how your partners contribute to your pipeline. Whether a partner sourced an opportunity or influenced it, attribution provides visibility into those contributions so you can measure impact and optimize collaboration.

### Where to Find Attribution in Crossbeam
In Crossbeam, click Attribution from the left-hand menu. This opens the Attribute Workspace, where you'll see a high-level overview and tools to explore opportunities in detail.


### All Opportunities Overview
At the top of the workspace, you’ll see a dashboard summarizing your opportunity data, including:
- Total Sourced: Opportunities that originated directly from a partner
- Total Influenced : Opportunities where a partner contributed after the opportunity was already in play
- Total Unattributed: Opportunities without partner involvement
- Time to Close with Partners: Average time it takes to close deals involving a partner Time to Close without Partners: A comparison metric for deals closed without partner involvement
- Time to Close without Partners: A comparison metric for deals closed without partner involvement
Filter and Customize Your View
You can tailor the All Opportunities view by:
- Searching for a specific account
- Applying filters for: Sales stage Partner Tags
- Sales stage
- Partner
- Tags
- Adjusting date ranges and deal amounts
To further customize the metrics shown, click the gear icon next to All Opportunities.


### Accounts Table
Below the All Opportunities overview, you'll find a table listing individual accounts. For each row, you'll see:
- Account name
- Assigned partner(s)
- Attribution type: Sourced, Influenced, or Review Attribution
- Deal amount
- Sales stage
- Closed date

#### Assign Attribution to a Partner
To review attribution, click on any account name in the table to open a side panel with more details. From here, you can:
- View the assigned partner
- Change or add the attribution type (Sourced or Influenced)
- Add or update attribution to include additional partners
Click on Activity to see deeper context for the account. The Activity Timeline shows partner interactions and signals that can guide your decision to mark a deal as sourced or influenced.

If Salesforce Attribution Push is configured, you will see a Salesforce link in the side panel. Click it to be redirected to your Salesforce instance.

Field Presets let you define which fields are visible to your partners and then reuse those settings across Populations and partners. They're especially useful for customizing access for different types of partners (like tech vs. channel).

What is a Field Preset?
A Field Preset is a saved set of fields that you choose to share with partners. Instead of configuring sharing one partner at a time, you can create a preset once and apply it broadly.

A Field Preset can be shared across multiple partners. Editing a preset changes the fields shared with everyone currently using it .

When can I use a preset? Presets are only available if a Population's sharing level is set to:
- Overlapping Accounts
- All Accounts
See Managing Population Settings for more details.
✍️ Note
Wherever you apply or adjust a field preset, creating, applying, bulk applying, or editing, you'll select it from a dropdown menu . Next to each preset option in the dropdown is a pencil icon, click it to edit that preset directly.


### How to Create a New Field Preset
You can create a Field Preset from several places in Crossbeam.

From any of the locations below, select Manage > Create Field Preset, then name the preset, select the fields you want to share, and click Create .

Option 1: From Data Sources
- Click the settings gear icon on a data source
- Click Field Presets

Option 2: From the Sharing Dashboard
- Click Partners in the navigation bar and select Sharing Dashboard
- Under Your Population Default Setting, click the pencil icon next to the Population
- Under Field Presets for Selected Population, expand the dropdown

Option 3: From Data > Populations
- Click Data in the navigation bar and select Populations
- Click the Partner-specific Sharing option from the three - dot menu in the Population's row
- Locate the Field Presets for the Selected Population

Option 4: From the Sharing Hub
- Click Data in the navigation bar and select Sharing Settings
- Click into a Population to see how the Population is being shared with partners
- Click the field preset in the row to edit


### How to See Which Partners Are Using a Preset
Before editing a preset, check who's currently assigned to it, since editing it will affect every partner using it.
- Click Data in the navigation bar and select Sharing Settings
- Click into the relevant Population
- Use the Field Preset filter to see everyone currently assigned to it
Additionally, you can check this per partner from the Partner List > Partner Detail Page > Settings .

### How to Review All Existing Presets for a Data Source
- Click the settings gear icon on a data source
- Click Field Presets to see the full list of presets created for that source


### How to Apply a Preset to a Partner
You can apply a preset to a partner when editing an existing partner's sharing from their Partner Detail Page or when first connecting with a new partner.

From the Sharing Dashboard
- Click Partners in the navigation bar and select Sharing Dashboard
- Under Sharing Settings with Partners, find the partner and click Edit in their row
- Adjust the field preset (via the dropdown) and sharing level as needed
From the Partner Detail Page
- Click Partners in the navigation bar and select the partner
- Go to Settings
- Adjust the field preset (via the dropdown) and sharing level as needed

From the Sharing Hub
- Click Data > Sharing Settings, then click into the relevant Population
- Locate the partner and click the Field Preset dropdown
- Click Manage to create a preset or select a preset for that partner
When Connecting with a New Partner
When accepting a new partner's invite, you can review and adjust your sharing settings before connecting. See Accept a Partner Invite for details.

### How to Bulk Apply a Preset to Multiple Partners
- Click Data > Populations
- Click the three dots menu next to the Population and select Partner Specific Sharing
- Check the box next to the partners, click Bulk Edit, and apply the same preset (via the dropdown) and sharing level at once
- Click Save


### How to Bulk Apply a Preset to Multiple Populations
- Click Data > Sharing Settings
- (Optional) Use the filter bar to narrow by Population type, sharing level, or field preset
- Select the Populations using the checkboxes
- Click Bulk Edit and apply the same preset (via the dropdown) and sharing level at once
- Click Save

✍️ Note Bulk editing Populations only changes each Population's default sharing level and preset. Partners who already have a custom override keep their existing settings.


### How to Edit an Existing Sharing Preset
‼️ Important
Before editing a Sharing Preset, check which partners are currently using it, see How to See Which Partners Are Using a Preset above. Editing a preset changes it for every partner assigned to it.
Open the field preset dropdown anywhere it appears (creating, applying, or reviewing presets), and click the pencil icon next to the preset you want to edit.
- Adjust the fields as needed
- Click Create to save the updated preset

To learn more about sharing settings, read Understanding Sharing Settings for Partners .

#### How to Delete a Sharing Preset
You can delete a Field Preset anywhere you can access the Manage button.
- Open the field preset dropdown and click Manage (or the pencil icon) next to the preset you want to delete
- Scroll down to Delete Preset
- Click Delete
✍️ Note Deleting a preset is permanent. Any Populations currently using the deleted preset will automatically switch to the Crossbeam Default preset.

📌 Starting August 19, 2026
Managed Offline Partners will remain available but will no longer receive data updates, and you will no longer be able to add new Managed Offline Partners.

We strongly recommend transitioning to Open Data Partners (ODPs). ODPs provide the most accurate, up-to-date third-party data layer sourced and maintained by Crossbeam.

There's no required migration or deadline to replace your Managed Offline Partners at this time, but since that data will become increasingly outdated over time, we recommend moving to Open Data Partners as soon as it's convenient for your team.

To learn more about Open Data Partners, see Open Data Partners .

When your most important partners aren't on Crossbeam, it's hard to know where you have overlap accounts with them. Managed Offline Partners solve this by giving you visibility into overlap with strategic ecosystem players, even if they're not part of your ecosystem network (Salesforce, AWS, Microsoft, SAP and Google Cloud). Using trusted, publicly available data curated by Crossbeam, these partner records behave like real partners in the platform. That means you can map your data, analyze overlaps, and take action, no partner invite required.
✍️ Note
Managed Offline Partners is available on all Crossbeam plans.

### How Managed Offline Partners Work
Managed Offline Partners are added to Crossbeam using verified, public third-party data sources . These accounts are fully maintained by Crossbeam to give you actionable insights without requiring the partner to join the platform.

How it works:
- Public data: Crossbeam sources customer lists from up to date verified public data using AI tools. Every data point has a traceable source to ensure a high level of confidence.
- Customer-only visibility: These partners include just one Population, Customers, so overlaps are based on mutual customers only
- No setup required: Crossbeam manages everything on the backend, no invites or onboarding needed
- Clearly marked: Icons, badges, and tooltips help you easily identify Managed Offline Partners throughout the platform
- Optional visibility: You can hide these partners from your Partners List as needed

- Go to the Partner List page. In the top right corner, click the arrow next to Add Partner, then select Add Managed Offline Partners .

See the Account Mapping Lists collection for details on building Account Mapping Lists in Crossbeam.

### FAQs
How often is the data updated? We update the Customer population for each Managed Offline Partner twice per year using a curated Google Sheet.

Where is the data sourced from? We pull from publicly available and verifiable sources (like customer pages or directories), using AI-powered tools to ensure data quality and coverage. Every data point has a traceable source to ensure a high level of confidence
This is the same type of information you'd find from sources like ZoomInfo, Crunchbase, HG Insights, or Partnerbase. We only include companies when we can confidently identify public evidence, such as customer logos on websites, case studies, job listings, or press releases, that validates a company as a customer of the partner in question. What makes Crossbeam unique is not the data itself. It's how we apply it. By infusing this public data into our platform, we enable account mapping across your pipeline and your broader partner ecosystem.

Can I hide these partners? Yes. If you prefer not to see them in your Partners List, you can hide Managed Offline Partners from view.

Can I invite these partners to join Crossbeam? Absolutely! These managed records are meant to give you immediate value, but you can still invite the partner to join the network if you'd like a fully connected experience.

What makes these different from standard partners?
- They're not real Crossbeam accounts
- Their data is sourced and maintained entirely by Crossbeam
- They only have one Population: Customers
- They're clearly labeled and separated from user-generated partners

Open Data Partners (ODPs) are curated technology vendors whose customer data is sourced and maintained by Crossbeam. ODPs let you identify account overlaps instantly, no partner invitation, connection, or data-sharing agreement required.

When you create a new Crossbeam account, one Open Data Partner is automatically added based on your ecosystem profile so you can start exploring overlap data immediately.
Open Data Partners are sourced and maintained by Crossbeam without requiring those vendors to be active Crossbeam users.


### Requirements
Open Data Partners are available on all Crossbeam plans. To add or manage ODPs, you must have:
- A Full Access seat
- A connected CRM
- Configured Populations
The number of ODPs available depends on your plan.
| Free | Connector | Supernode | Enterprise |
| 1 ODP | 1 ODP | 3 ODPs Includes product-level segmentation | 10 ODPs Includes product-level segmentation |
✍️ Note
If you’re on a Free plan and want to swap the ODP Crossbeam assigned during onboarding, contact the support team .

Connector ODP limits are included with updated Connector plans that have a platform fee. If you’re on an earlier Connector package, you can add the platform capabilities to your subscription to get this limit. Contact your account team or support team for details.

On all other plans, you choose your own ODPs, so swapping does not apply. Once you reach your plan’s limit, you’ll see options to upgrade or contact Sales.

### How Open Data Partners work
Open Data Partners are partner records maintained by Crossbeam. Unlike standard Crossbeam partners, ODPs require no mutual connection or data-sharing agreement, and Crossbeam refreshes the underlying data continuously.

ODPs appear throughout Crossbeam with a dedicated Open Data Partner icon/badge so you can tell them apart from your connected partners. This includes:
- Partner List
- Search
- Partner Detail pages
- Account Mapping Lists
With an Open Data Partner, you can:
- Map your Populations, Customers, Prospects, Open Opportunities, against the ODP
- See overlap counts, Potential Revenue (depending on plan), and Partner Score (depending on plan), the same as any partner
- Build and organize Lists of overlapping accounts
- See which of a vendor's specific products a shared account uses (Supernode and Enterprise plans only)
- Hide an ODP from your Partner List
ODPs include a Customer Population only, allowing you to see which accounts are customers of that vendor.


#### See Product-Level Data
(Available on Supernode and Enterprise plans)
Open Data Partners also include Product-level segmentation, the specific products a shared account uses within that vendor's portfolio. For example, instead of a single is a customer of Microsoft signal, you see which parts of the Microsoft portfolio that account has actually adopted.

Where to find it
Open a shared account from any Account Mapping List built against an Open Data Partner.

Products appear on the account's detail view alongside the overlap data.

Not all ODPs will have multiple products available, as some vendors have one product offering.
✍️ Note
When filtering the Products column, "is" only returns accounts where that's the only product listed. The filter will not match accounts that have that product plus other products. To find every account with a specific product regardless of what else is listed, use "contains" instead.

For example, filtering Products is "AWS Lambda" only returns accounts where AWS Lambda is the sole product; filtering Products contains "AWS Lambda" returns every account that includes it, even alongside other products.
Use this product-level segmentation to:
- Target accounts running a specific product rather than the vendor as a whole
- Tailor outreach to the part of a vendor's portfolio an account already uses
- Spot accounts using a product that pairs with yours
- Prioritize co-sell conversations where the product fit is strongest

### Where ODP data comes from
Crossbeam sources and maintains ODP data from publicly available “is-a-customer-of” signals, partner directories, tech stack listings, vendor marketplaces, public case studies, and integration pages.
This data:
- Requires no action from the partner
- Is not shared by the vendor through Crossbeam
- Is not sourced from your data or any other Crossbeam customer's data
- Is held entirely separate from Crossbeam Network data

### If you have Managed Offline Partners
Existing Managed Offline Partners remain in place, and reports and Lists with Managed Offline Partners will continue to work.

Managed Offline Partners are now deprecated, which means:
- Crossbeam no longer refreshes their underlying data
- You keep access to everything you have today
- They don't count toward your plan's Open Data Partner (ODP) limit

#### Replace a Managed Offline Partner
Deprecated Managed Offline Partners have a Replace with Open Data Partner option. You can replace partners individually or in bulk.

Replacing a Managed Offline Partner gives you:
- Continuously refreshed data
- Product-level detail on Supernode and Enterprise plans
⚠️ Replacing is permanent
Replacing a Managed Offline Partner deletes the original record and everything associated with it, including Lists,Tags, and Populations. It cannot be restored or re-added.

The replacement uses one of your ODP allocations and can't be swapped for another ODP .

If you're not ready to permanently replace a Managed Offline Partner, keep it. The ODP will provide continuously refreshed data while your existing Managed Offline Partner remains available.

Replacing your Managed Offline Partner for an Open Data Partner gives you continuously refreshed data and, on Supernode and Enterprise, product-level detail.

### FAQs
Is my Crossbeam data used to build Open Data Partners? No. Your CRM data, Populations, overlaps, and Lists are never used to build an ODP record and are never visible to anyone else.

Can I get product-level data on my Managed Offline Partners? No. Products are available on Open Data Partners only. If you have a Managed Offline Partner for a vendor with an equivalent ODP, you can replace it to get product-level data.

Will Open Data Partners replace my partner relationships? No. ODPs complement your partner program by providing immediate overlap data while you build direct partner connections.

How is ODP data sourced? ODP data is publicly sourced ' is-a-customer-of ' signals, partner directories, tech stack listings, marketplaces, public case studies, and integration pages. Crossbeam independently maintains these datasets and refreshes them daily.

Can I swap Open Data Partners? Free plans that had an ODP automatically allocated during onboarding can swap once. On other plans, you select your own Open Data Partners when you add them. If you’ve hit your limit, reach out to our team for steps on how to add additional ODPs.

Why can I only see Customer overlaps? ODPs are based on customer datasets sourced and maintained by Crossbeam. Prospect data is not currently included.

Q. I see an error message like this, and I am nervous about syncing in more data to Crossbeam: ​
A: Any new fields you sync to Crossbeam are yours only - this data is not shared with your partners. This data allows Crossbeam to accurately reflect the actual revenue impacts of your Partners.

A: Attributed revenue from this opportunity will no longer count towards your statistics or those partner(s).

Q. What happens if an Opportunity has an empty amount or close date?
A: Attributed revenue from opportunities without amount or close date are excluded from the denominator when calculating Roll-Up Metrics. ​

- Crossbeam Attributions
- Crossbeam Attributions with Account
- Crossbeam Attributions with Opportunity
- Crossbeam Attributions with Partner
Q. Similarly to opportunities that get pushed into Salesforce today, does each attribution record connect to an account and an opportunity?
A. Yes. Each attribution is related to the account (i.e. the account you closed) and the partner account (if you include it during the setup of the integration).

Q. When configuring the Attribution Push in Crossbeam Core Settings, what does “Add a Partner ID from Salesforce” do?
A. If you input your Partner Account IDs here, your attributions will be linkable to the Partner in your Salesforce instead of by their text name only.
