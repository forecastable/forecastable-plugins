## What is the Crossbeam tab in Salesforce? (HC 11501777)
URL: https://help.crossbeam.com/en/articles/11501777-access-crossbeam-deal-navigator-inside-salesforce

In this article:
- What is the Crossbeam tab in Salesforce?
- What features can I see within Salesforce?
- How to Set up the Crossbeam tab in Salesforce

The Crossbeam SFDC Iframe is an embedded view (called iframe), or tab, that brings Crossbeam features, Deal Navigator and the Performance Dashboard, directly in your Salesforce environment. This means you don’t need to leave Salesforce to leverage Crossbeam’s ecosystem intelligence data.
✍️ Note
The Crossbeam tab in Salesforce is available to any Salesforce user once the Crossbeam managed package is installed.
- Additional Crossbeam features, such as reports and dashboards, require the Salesforce custom object to be enabled
- Access to Deal Navigator in Crossbeam (app.crossbeam.com) still requires a Crossbeam user seat.


### What features can I see on the Iframe?
The Crossbeam tab displays two tools:

1. Deal Navigator: A table view of open opportunities that overlap with your partners’ customers. It helps users focus on the most promising deals and partnerships.
Sales managers and individual sellers can enhance sales efficiency by leveraging Ecosystem Intelligence to prioritize deals and surface key partner insights. Learn more.

2. The Performance dashboard tab: Designed to measure and maximize the impact of ecosystem-led initiatives.
This data-driven dashboard helps your team to:
- Measure ecosystem coverage on open opportunities
- Analyze ecosystem influence on closed deals
- Track team engagement with Crossbeam data
Learn more about the Performance Dashboard here .


### How to Set up the Crossbeam tab in Salesforce
In Salesforce Lightning:
- Go to Setup → App Manager
- Find your Sales app and click Edit
- In the Navigation Items section, add the Crossbeam tab (this is where the iframe will live)
- Save your changes
In Salesforce Classic:
- Click the + icon in your tab bar
- Select Customize My Tabs
- Choose your current app from the dropdown
- Add the Crossbeam tab to your visible tabs
- Save
This will ensure Deal Navigator and the Performance Dashboard are easily accessible directly within your team's workspace.

### Universal Copilot Settings in Crossbeam
To customize what Partner data is available in Salesforce, you will need to configure the Copilot settings in your Crossbeam account.
✍️ Important
Configuring the Universal Copilot Settings will apply to all Crossbeam Copilots attached to your Crossbeam account. This includes Copilot for Chrome, Copilot for Salesforce, Copilot for Outreach, Copilot for HubSpot, and Copilot for Gong (also known as Gong Engage).
From the Integrations workspace in Crossbeam, locate the Crossbeam Copilot for Salesforce row, and click the Settings button to open a side drawer.

In the settings drawer, customize your data push by unchecking any of your data or your partner data you do not want pushed into the Crossbeam Copilot. Additionally, you can uncheck specific partners from the data push.

Click the Save button when you are done.

✍️ Note
Configuring Universal settings does not count towards your record export limits.

### Sales Settings in Crossbeam
Seats required: Sales seat (Manager role) or Full Access seat (Admin) · Connector or Supernode plan

Sales Settings is where to configure everything your team can access, such as enabled partners and account data, co-selling templates, managing Slack shared channels, and notifications.

Step 1: Access Sales Settings
- From the Crossbeam navigation, go to Settings → Sales Settings
Step 2: Enable partners
- Follow the setup prompts under Partner and Account Data
- Check the boxes for the partners you want your team to access, and click continue
- Select an account status for each partners custom Population and click continue
- Click the Confirm and Import Data button
- Manage enabled Partners by clicking on Enabled Partners
- From the partner view, you can: Configure Inbox Settings, including enabling or disabling Direct Shared Conversations Use the three-dot menu to edit co-selling templates, manage partner settings, or hide partner data Assign or update the Partner Manager Add collateral links View partner requests Review overlapping accounts and send messages
- Configure Inbox Settings, including enabling or disabling Direct Shared Conversations
- Use the three-dot menu to edit co-selling templates, manage partner settings, or hide partner data
- Assign or update the Partner Manager
- Add collateral links
- View partner requests
- Review overlapping accounts and send messages
✍️ Note
If a partner isn't appearing under Enabled Partners, they haven't been activated yet. Click Manage under Crossbeam to search for and add them.
Step 3: Set up co-selling templates
Co-selling templates define the questions your sales reps can ask partners and the context they should provide before starting a conversation. Setting up templates also enables Get Intel for your sellers.

Access Co-Selling Template settings
- Go to Settings → Sales Settings → Co-Selling Templates
- Click Edit Templates, add your question text, and optionally use Limit to Specific Partners to restrict it to certain partners
- Toggle Allow Custom Questions on if you want reps to add their own questions
- Click Continue
- Next, type talking points directly into the context table
- Click +Add for each new prompt
- Click Save Changes when done
Step 4: Set up Shared Slack Channels
- Go to Settings → Sales Settings, click Add to Slack, and follow the prompts to install the app
- Once installed, click Manage Slack Shared Channels and select the correct Slack channel for each partner from the dropdown
- Click Save Changes when done
✍️ Note
For private Slack channels, the Crossbeam Slack app must be added to the channel directly in Slack via channel name → Integrations → Apps before it will appear in the dropdown. Your partner must also have fully joined the channel first.
Step 5: Configure alerts
- Go to Settings → Sales Settings → Alerts, toggle notification types on or off
- Click Save Changes
✍️ Note
If Slack is not configured, all notifications default to email.

### Pipeline Generation
Seats required: Sales seat or Full Access seat · Connector or Supernode plan (Explorer: limited access)

Pipeline Generation helps your sales team find warm, high-potential net-new accounts based on ecosystem connections. Instead of cold outreach, it surfaces Top Prospects, accounts that overlap with your partners and match your ICP, so sellers can prioritize leads that already have built-in trust and relevance.
Sellers can filter by Account Owner, Industry, Number of Employees, Partners, and Country, or sort by Recent Partner Customer to see accounts that have just become a partner's customer, a strong buying intent signal.

Available in Crossbeam and in Salesforce.

### Performance Dashboard
Seats required: Full Access seat only · Supernode plan

The Performance Dashboard gives sales leaders and partnership managers a unified view of how your partner ecosystem impacts pipeline, revenue, and sales engagement. Use it to:
- Understand how partners influence pipeline and revenue
- Identify reps effectively using ecosystem data
- Spot missed opportunities for partner engagement
- Demonstrate the ecosystem's value across your organization
The dashboard is organized into four tabs:
- Open : generated pipeline metrics and open opportunities with partner overlap
- Closed : closed-won opportunities influenced by partners
- Team: team engagement activity across the ecosystem
- Partner: partner performance and leaderboard

The free plan (Explorer) does not have Sales seats. However, there is a limited preview of Copilot for Salesforce, which does not require a Sales seat.

To upgrade your account, visit the Plan & Billing page .

✍️ Note
Feature access and functionality may vary based on the permissions configured by your organization's Crossbeam admin. If anything outlined in this article does not function as expected, contact your Crossbeam admin for further assistance.

If you are a Crossbeam admin setting up sales features for your team, see Crossbeam Sales Settings and Features Overview .

### Log in to Crossbeam
Your Crossbeam admin assigns you a Sales seat before you can log in.

Via email invite:
- Click Join Now in your invite email
- Create your account and verify your email address
- Log in at app.crossbeam.com and accept the invite to join your organization
Via SSO:
- Your admin pre-provisions your access, no email invite required
- Contact your Crossbeam admin if you don't have your organization's SSO login URL
✍️ Note
If you attempt to log in via SSO and receive an error, contact your Crossbeam admin to confirm your seat has been assigned.

### Log in to Crossbeam Copilot
If your organization uses SSO:
- Open Crossbeam Copilot in your tool (Salesforce, HubSpot, Chrome, Gong, or Outreach)
- Click Continue with SSO to gain access automatically, no separate invite needed
If your organization does not use SSO:
- You will receive a separate email invite for Copilot access
- Click Join Now and follow the prompts to set up your account
If you don't have a Sales seat yet:
- If your admin has enabled Copilot for Salesforce, you'll see a preview version with limited data
- Click Request Access in the Copilot panel, an email is sent to your Crossbeam admin to assign you a seat
- Your admin can review pending seat requests under Settings → Team →Seat Request tab

### Message a Partner
Sales seat or Full Access seat · Connector or Supernode plan

Get Intel is how users request partner help on a specific account, an introduction, background intel, or co-sell assist. It is powered by co-selling templates set up by your admin in Sales Settings.

Where to access Get Intel:
- Deal Navigator: Click the Message button in the partner column on any open opportunity
- Pipeline Generation: Click the Message button in the table
- Crossbeam Copilot: Click the Get Intel button on a partner in the Copilot panel
✍️ Note
If the Get Intel button does not display, that partner hasn't been enabled for co-selling by your admin. Contact your Crossbeam admin for assistance.
Managing all your conversations:
- Navigate to Messages from the left-hand navigation to view all open and completed partner conversations across all partners in one place
Starting a conversation:
- Click Get Intel/Message at your entry point
- Select questions from your admin's co-selling template
- Add context for the partner and send
What you can do in a conversation:
- Reply to and complete conversations with partners
- View overlapping accounts associated with that partner
- Mark Attribution on closed deals to credit the partner assist, this syncs automatically to Salesforce
- View collateral links your admin has added for that partner
✍️ Note
Standard role users can initiate new partner requests, reply, complete conversations, and mark Attribution.
Limited role users can reply to and complete conversations but cannot initiate new requests.
Contact your Crossbeam admin if you're unsure of your role.

### Notification Settings
Sales seat or Full Access seat · Connector or Supernode plan
Crossbeam notifies you of new partner overlaps, deal updates, and partner activity via email or Slack.

Notification Preferences: Manage your personal notification preferences by navigating to Settings → Profile & Preferences → Notifications and toggling individual notification types on or off. Covers Deal Insights emails, new overlap notifications, Slack nudge messages, weekly reports, and action required reminders.

List Notifications : Configure directly on any List by clicking the bell icon . Sends overlap updates via email or Slack.
