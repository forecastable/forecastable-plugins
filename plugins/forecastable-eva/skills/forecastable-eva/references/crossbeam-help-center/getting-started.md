Crossbeam AI is your in-app conversational assistant, powered by the proprietary partner data that lives in your ecosystem. It delivers trusted answers and lets you take action instantly.

Crossbeam AI is a role-aware assistant embedded directly in the Crossbeam app. It turns ecosystem intelligence like partner overlaps, influence signals, and ELG playbooks into natural language interactions, so you can ask a question, get a personalized response, and act on it immediately.

### What Can Crossbeam AI do?
- Retrieve partner overlap insights and influence signals directly from your partner data
- Answer tactical and strategic questions like: “Which deals have partner overlap but no activity?” “Draft an intro message for Partner X.”
- “Which deals have partner overlap but no activity?”
- “Draft an intro message for Partner X.”
- Recommend next best actions and execute them, like: Create a smart list Send a Slack message Trigger a Salesforce update
- Create a smart list
- Send a Slack message
- Trigger a Salesforce update
- Surface relevant ELG playbook guidance or help documentation based on your question

#### Use Cases
- Streamline workflows: no need to click through multiple tools, just ask a question and get what you need
- Accelerate sales cycles: surface ecosystem insights at key deal stages and take immediate action
- Enable every team member: sales, partnerships, or operations, Crossbeam AI tailors responses based on your role and permissions

### How it Works
- Location : Look for the Crossbeam AI icon in the top-right corner of the app
- Powered by : A large language model (LLM), with OpenAI as a subprocessor
- Data access : Your partner data (e.g., overlaps, attribution fields, CRM context) Crossbeam ELG playbooks Help center content and documentation
- Your partner data (e.g., overlaps, attribution fields, CRM context)
- Crossbeam ELG playbooks
- Help center content and documentation
- Publicly available data: We use data such as job postings, company websites, and other public sources to help determine customer lists for these companies. We strive for accuracy within the limits of this public data.
- Integrated with : Slack, Salesforce (where applicable), and Lists (beta)
- Role-aware : Adapts its answers based on user type (e.g., Sales Seat vs. Full Access)

### FAQs
Is customer data used to train the LLM?
No. Customer data is not used to train the LLM (large language model) we use.

Is my data secure? Yes. Crossbeam AI only accesses data from your account and uses OpenAI as a subprocessor under strict security and compliance measures.

Does Crossbeam AI take action automatically? Only when prompted. It may suggest actions, but you stay in control of what gets executed.

Is it available to all users? AI Chat is available on all Crossbeam plans. Access may vary based on your plan and seat type.

## Overview (HC 15507121)
URL: https://help.crossbeam.com/en/articles/15507121-onboarding-lookalike-prospects-population

In this article:
- Overview
- How it Works
- Using Lookalike Prospects
- FAQ
Lookalike Prospects is an automatically generated Population of prospect accounts created when you sign up for Crossbeam. Using your company's domain and data that is sourced and maintained by Crossbeam, Crossbeam identifies accounts that resemble your ICP and adds them to your workspace.

This allows you to start exploring account mapping immediately, before connecting a CRM or inviting partners.
To learn more about the onboarding experience for new Crossbeam accounts, see this Onboarding Guide Article .

### How It Works
When you create a new Crossbeam account, Crossbeam automatically:
- Verifies your company domain
- Identifies similar accounts using customer data sourced and maintained by Crossbeam
- Creates a Lookalike Prospects Population
- Maps those accounts against an Open Data Partner (ODP)

Your first view in Crossbeam is an overlap report showing which of your lookalike prospects are customers of the selected Open Data Partner.
No setup is required.

If Crossbeam cannot confidently generate a tailored list, the Population may appear as Sample Prospects . Both Populations function the same way.

### Using Your Lookalike Prospects
Your Lookalike Prospects Population behaves like any other custom Population in Crossbeam. You can:
- View it in Populations
- Filter accounts in Lists
- Run overlap reports with partners
- Edit or delete the Population
The Population remains available after onboarding is complete.
Lookalike Prospects is a one-time snapshot and does not automatically refresh.

### Frequently Asked Questions
What are Open Data Partners? Open Data Partners (ODPs) are curated technology vendors whose customer data is sourced and maintained by Crossbeam. ODPs let you identify account overlaps instantly, no partner invitation, connection, or data-sharing agreement required.

Does the Population update automatically?
No. Lookalike Prospects is generated once during signup and does not sync or refresh.

Can I see overlaps immediately?
Yes. Crossbeam automatically maps the Population against an Open Data Partner and displays the results as your first list.

Why do I see Sample Prospects instead?
Sample Prospects is generated when Crossbeam cannot confidently create a tailored lookalike list. It functions the same way as Lookalike Prospects.

Can I delete the Population?
Yes. Navigate to Populations and delete it like any custom Population.

## Crossbeam (HC 3160166)
URL: https://help.crossbeam.com/en/articles/3160166-glossary-of-key-terminology-in-crossbeam

Crossbeam, the leading Ecosystem-Led Growth SaaS platform, enables secure identification of shared customers and prospects with partner organizations. It seamlessly integrates with third-party platforms, allowing precise control over shared information through flexible matching and sharing rules, while maintaining data privacy.

### Data Sources
Crossbeam automatically syncs data from the most common systems of record about customers and sales prospects, like Salesforce, HubSpot, and a number of custom data sources . We also support CSV uploads .
✍️ Note
To check if your data source is supported, click here to visit the Integration collection in the Help Center.

### Data Sharing Rules
Crossbeam users have precise control over what data is shared and when. Our flexible solution to data sharing makes this possible, giving users control over what conditions need to be met in order for data to be exposed, and what data is shared when it is.

### Data Sharing Requests
The Request Data feature allows you to select what data you’d like to receive from your established partners and send them a request within Crossbeam.

### Integrations
Export overlaps data from Crossbeam to a number of destinations. Data can be pushed into your Salesforce instance, exported to a CSV, or accessed programmatically via our REST API .

Read more about integrations here .

### Overlaps
An Overlap is a match between a record in your Population and a Population of your partner. Data Sharing uses Overlaps as a condition for exposing data, and Reports use Overlaps to show specific intersections between data sets.

Learn how to create reports and view overlaps .

Learn how overlaps impact your billing tier.

### Partners
Your Partners are the companies that you collaborate with on Crossbeam. You can only establish a partnership via our double opt-in invite system . Features like data sharing, and overlap analysis are only available to companies who have established a partnership.

### Populations
In the Crossbeam app, a Population is a segment of data containing people or companies. Populations are the basis for data sharing and often map to funnel stages like leads, qualified opportunities, and customers.

Crossbeam offers an intuitive interface for users to build and refine their Populations, and the data in those Populations is automatically kept up to date by our data integrations.

### Record Exports
Record Exports allow users to transfer enriched data from Crossbeam, gathered through account mapping activities, and transfer it to external platforms, such as data warehouses (e.g., Snowflake), integrations, and CRMs for further analysis.

Each record is counted once per subscription term, regardless of the number of integrations, destinations, partners, or overlaps. Even if a record undergoes multiple updates within that subscription term, it still counts only once.

#### User Roles
User Roles determine access levels within Crossbeam and Crossbeam for Sales, defining what features and data each user can access. Roles are customizable to suit the needs of different team members.
