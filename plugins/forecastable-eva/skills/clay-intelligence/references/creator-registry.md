- Last new item = age of the newest video from that creator in this knowledge base, as YouTube labelled it on 2026-10-04 (relative, approximate). It is not a full audit of the channel's latest upload unless stated.
- Empty (0) = number of consecutive monthly checks that found nothing new and relevant. All start at 0.
- Where no channel handle was captured, the link is the video used; open the channel from there.
