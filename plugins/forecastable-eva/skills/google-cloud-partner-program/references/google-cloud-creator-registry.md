# Google Cloud creator and source registry
Version: 2026.09 (built 2026-09-26). Walked monthly by the refresh task.
Rules: rank by usefulness to a partner team turning this hyperscaler into pipeline, not audience size.
Two consecutive empty checks move a creator to Dormant (checked quarterly). New creators need one
substantive, current, program-specific item and a stated reason. Label: OFFICIAL, VENDOR, INDEPENDENT,
CREATOR. "Last checked" is the date actually fetched.

Important 2026 context for this registry: two of the brief's named Google voices have left Google. Colleen Kapase departed Google Cloud in March 2026 and joined OpenAI; Philip Larson joined OpenAI in July 2026. Their Google-era content is still the best explanation of Partner Network design, but they no longer speak for Google. David Smith (Head of Global Partner Program since March 2026) is the new voice to watch. See Tier 1 and Dormant.
